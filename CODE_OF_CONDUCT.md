# Code of Conduct

This circle follows the same four attitudes that both Patanjali (Yoga Sutra 1.33) and the Buddha teach: **friendliness, compassion, gladness for others' good, and equanimity.**

## We commit to

- **Kindness** — speak in ways that are true, helpful, timely and kind.
- **Respect for all traditions** — Hindu, Buddhist, Bön, other faiths and none. Disagree with ideas, never demean people.
- **Inclusion** — everyone is welcome regardless of age, body, ability, ethnicity, nationality, caste, gender, sexuality, religion, experience or background.
- **Consent and privacy** — never share another person's private information, practice experiences or messages without permission.
- **Honesty** — no impersonation, no fake credentials, no claims of special spiritual status to gain influence.
- **Safety** — no medical, psychological or dangerous practice advice presented as certain; point people to professionals when needed.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or hate speech.
- Sexual content or advances.
- Recruiting members to groups, paid courses or teachers through this project, or asking members for money.
- Pressuring anyone to accept a belief, teacher or practice.
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainers privately (see the repository owner's GitHub profile), or use GitHub's **Report content** option. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or block people who break this code.

When someone causes harm, we follow Milarepa's example: acknowledge it, make amends, and change. Repair is always welcome.

## If you are in distress

This community cares, but it is not a crisis service. Please contact emergency services or a crisis line: Lifeline **13 11 14** (Australia), **988** (United States), or your local equivalent.
